## Leads worth chasing

- **MobyGames entries** for Backpacker 2 (https://www.mobygames.com/game/11520) and Backpacker 3 returned 403. They likely hold the fullest PC mechanic description, screenshots, and possibly transport/time details.
- **Contemporary Swedish reviews:** Svenska Dagbladet 27 Sep 2003 ("Lärorik fotvandring är inne på åttonde året"), Aftonbladet 24 Oct 2003 ("Res jorden runt på din dator") and Expressen 22 Oct 2003. These are primary sources for how quiz and jobs were received ("lärorik" = educational framing).
- **The MAG Interactive 2017 press release** (archived copy via the Wayback Machine) would establish the mobile release and business partnership.
- **Google Play listing** "Backpacker™ Travel Quiz Trivia" (com.qiiwi.backpacker?). The fetch was truncated, so release date and review text are still missing.
- **The "Rosetta Stone resale" memory** suggests a trading mechanic in Backpacker 2. Worth confirming via longplay videos or MobyGames.
- **Backpacker™ Go!** (Qiiwi, App Store id6478389736) is described as a board-game spin-off. Not investigated.
- **Swedish/Norwegian forums** (Flashback, FZ.se, Reddit r/sweden, r/norge) for nostalgia threads on quiz-as-schoolwork and repetitiveness.

## Looked for but could not find

- **PC transport modes** (plane/train/bus) and their cost/time trade-offs: no source retrieved this run describes them.
- **Explicit PC time/day budget:** Wikipedia says only "in the shortest time". No source gave a day counter, deadline or race opponent.
- **Passport stamps in the PC games:** confirmed only for the mobile game. No PC source mentions them.
- **How jobs paid in the PC games:** jobs and questions are linked, and "certificates" exist, but no source gave the pay formula or said whether wrong answers cost money.
- **Quiz content by category:** no breakdown of question topics (geography vs. job-specific) for the PC games beyond "job questions" and "city and country questions" on mobile.
- **Was the quiz seen as fun or schoolwork:** no direct evidence. The only signals are the "lärorikt" (educational) framing used positively by mobile reviewers and the SvD headline, plus one complaint about repetitive job questions.
- **A distinct browser/web Backpacker game** from the 2010s: none found. The search surfaced only the Qiiwi mobile app.
- **Mobile saving and session length:** not described on the App Store page.
- **Pan Interactive as publisher:** no source supports it. Sources name BMG Interactive (Backpacker 1–2) and Pan Vision AB (Backpacker 3 onwards).
- **English-language contemporary reviews** of Backpacker 2: none surfaced.
