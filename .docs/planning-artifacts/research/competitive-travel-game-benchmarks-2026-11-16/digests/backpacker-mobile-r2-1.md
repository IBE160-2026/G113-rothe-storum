## Claims

1. Sleeping on the street is free and still restores some energy. A hotel gives more energy per coin and carries no robbery risk. The guide calls the street "a fallback when you're out of both money and energy." This means having no coins does not end the run: the player can still sleep. — "Backpacker mobile game cheats, tips & strategy guide…" — TouchTapPlay — Oct 2017 (via search snippet) — https://www.touchtapplay.com/backpacker-mobile-game-cheats-tips-strategy-guide-to-answer-all-questions/ (also https://touchtapplay.com/?p=18592) — accessed 2026-11-16 — confidence: medium (fan guide, page returned 403, content seen only through search summaries) — class: mechanic — confirms and extends the App Store page.
2. "The game gives you the option to sleep on the street for free but it comes with natural risks such as getting robbed." "You also do not get as much energy if you sleep on the street." — "Backpacker (iOS) Cheats, Tips & Tricks to Answer All Questions and Conquer the World" — LevelWinner (Matt Dahlstrom) — 2017-10-18 — https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=11989 — accessed 2026-11-16 — confidence: medium-high (an independent second source that agrees with claim 1) — class: mechanic — confirms the App Store page ("risk sleeping in the streets") and names the risk as robbery plus less energy.
3. Out of money AND out of energy: "would rarely happen but it is still a possibility." The suggested remedy is "to just wait until the next day before playing the game again… you can claim your daily bonus and hopefully that would be enough to get you out of trouble." This means the soft lock is resolved by waiting for a real-time daily bonus. It is not game over. — LevelWinner, as in claim 2 — confidence: medium — class: mechanic.
4. Energy does not refill over time. It is restored only by sleeping, either in a paid hotel or on the free street. — TouchTapPlay (via search snippet), as in claim 1 — confidence: low-medium (only one source, seen only as a snippet) — class: mechanic.
5. The App Store sells an in-app purchase named "Keep playing" for $0.99. The listing does not say what it does. Other in-app purchases: Small stack $1.99, Wallet $2.99, Medium stack $3.99, Backpacker Premium $3.49 (weekly), Sack of Coins $7.99, Large stack $13.99, Small chest $13.99, Huge chest $26.99, Case $28.99. The listing says "Contains Advertising". Rating: 4.5/5 from 5.4K US ratings. — Backpacker™ on the App Store (US) — Apple / Qiiwi Games AB — unknown — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/backpacker/id711435089 — accessed 2026-11-16 — confidence: high for the list, unknown for what "Keep playing" does — class: mechanic/monetisation — confirms the App Store page (Premium, coin purchases, ads).
6. Premium: "Some items in the app require Backpacker Premium, such as no waiting time when traveling." Premium is a weekly auto-renewing subscription at $3.49. — US App Store, as in claim 5 — confidence: high — class: mechanic — confirms the App Store page.
7. Travel costs coins: "In order to move from city to city you have to buy airline tickets and that takes lots of coins" (user review, debbielou55). LevelWinner adds that some tickets are cheaper but take longer, which is a trade-off between price and waiting time. — US App Store review, and LevelWinner — confidence: medium — class: mechanic.
8. Jobs are the main source of income. Upgrading the wallet raises job bonuses. Some jobs are minigames rather than trivia. Passport missions unlock new areas. Chests open on a timer using keys earned from questions and jobs. — LevelWinner, and TouchTapPlay (snippet) — confidence: medium — class: mechanic — confirms the App Store page (jobs, minigames, passport).
9. Release date. The aggregator says it was "first launched in 30th November 2016" (possibly a soft launch; this is unverified). The MAG Interactive press release says the game launched on the App Store and Google Play on 2017-10-12, and that a Facebook web version followed on 2017-12-12, with MAG named as publisher. — "MAG Interactive: Backpacker™ now launched on Facebook" — MAG Interactive via Cision — 2017-12-12 — https://news.cision.com/mag-interactive/r/mag-interactive--backpacker---now-launched-on-facebook-,c2412328 — accessed 2026-11-16 — confidence: high for the October 2017 global launch, low for the 2016 date (source: game-solver.com, https://game-solver.com/backpacker/) — class: release-fact. This resolves the "2013/2016/2017" question: global publisher launch October 2017, a likely earlier soft launch in 2016 (unverified), and app ID 711435089 suggests the listing was created around 2013 (my inference, unverified).
10. MAG Interactive and WeAreQiiwi announced a publishing partnership in September 2017. MAG handled localisation, marketing and publishing. Qiiwi later ended the publisher agreement. — Search snippets from maginteractive.com (URL now 404), qiiwi.com/press-releases/24, and Börsvärlden — confidence: medium (seen only through snippets; dates of the agreement's end unconfirmed) — class: release-fact — confirms the co-release hypothesis.
11. 2 million registered players by 2018-09-04, mainly in the USA, UK, Germany, Sweden, Norway and Denmark. The CEO says the company will focus on new content and on scaling through organic, viral and paid traffic. — "WeAreQiiwis speltitel Backpacker har passerat 2 miljoner spelare" — Börsvärlden/Finwire — 2018-09-04 — https://borsvarlden.com/artiklar/weareqiiwis-speltitel-backpacker-har-passerat-2-miljoner-spelare — accessed 2026-11-16 — confidence: high — class: release-fact — confirms the App Store page / brief.

Google Play: 4.1/5 from about 26.9K reviews, about 1M+ installs, last update 2025-11-12. — appgoblin.info / apppricinglab (via search snippet), https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.qiiwi.backpacker — confidence: low-medium (third-party trackers; the Play page itself did not load) — class: release-fact.
13. End state. There is no hard time limit or game over in any source found. Players report finishing the content: "all 69 cities! This game is fun but limited", "we have all the cities, all five stars and nowhere else to go", "you get to the end and that it it." The game has an in-game day cycle ("in a matter of two in game days") and timed multiplayer challenges ("the game cuts us off at 3-4 hours before the challenge ends"). — game-solver.com (aggregated App Store reviews) — unknown — https://game-solver.com/backpacker/ — confidence: low-medium (user reviews passed through an aggregator) — class: mechanic/player-sentiment.
14. Players complain about a paywall and grind: "You reach a point where you can go no further unless you start paying"; "Enough coins required that you would either have to play for days or part with quite a bit of real money"; "coin rewards are so small it is hard to stay in a city and earn those"; one player even complains about paying "to sleep on the street". This last one contradicts the guides' claim that the street is free, possibly because of a later change. — game-solver.com reviews — confidence: low — class: player-sentiment.
15. Players complain about ads: "the constant click-watch an ad repeat takes away from anything positive"; "it just gets more ad heavy." — game-solver.com reviews — confidence: low — class: player-sentiment — confirms the App Store page (ads).
16. Players complain about repetition and getting stuck: "hanging around answering repeated questions" (cmc056); "I end up sitting in a city after I have accomplished all tasks on my passport for days" (debbielou55); questions are "super USA specific"; crashes and loading hangs when changing city. — US App Store reviews, and game-solver.com — confidence: medium (on Apple's own page) / low (aggregator) — class: player-sentiment.
17. Players praise the game as educational and addictive: "highly educational but so fun you don't realize you're learning." One iOS reviewer says it is possible to finish the game and travel to new cities without any in-app purchase (via search snippet). — game-solver.com, and App Store snippet — confidence: low — class: player-sentiment. This conflicts with claim 14 and shows that player opinion on the paywall is split.
