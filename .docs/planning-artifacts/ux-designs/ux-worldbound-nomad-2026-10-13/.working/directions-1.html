WORLDBOUND: NOMAD · UX design directions · round 1

Four visual directions for the map and city day

Each column is a full visual personality, not just a palette swap. Each one shows the map screen (Paris, Day 5, London tapped), a strip of the other colour mode, the city day in Paris, and Jonas's out-of-money state in Rome with Call home. Scroll sideways to compare and down to see every screen. Each file also opens on its own.

  • The map style is part of the choice: geographic + loupe (A), low-poly (B), schematic metro (C), illustrated rounded (D).
  • In all four, stamped cities show a check or stamp shape, and locked routes show a lock and a written reason, so nothing relies on colour alone.

Transit Line

The journey as a metro map. Cities are stations, and each transport mode is its own line style: dotted plane, double-rail train, dashed bus. Deck colours on cream with table-like sheets make it the densest and most information-first option, and comparing prices is effortless.
