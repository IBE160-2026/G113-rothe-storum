Direction A2 · light primary · merged

Sunrise Expedition, merged

Direction A stays the base: warm paper, navy ink and ochre, the geographic map with the Europe loupe. These pieces come in from the other directions:

  • The logo is just WORLDBOUND, with the compass-rose O and a mountain line. NOMAD is gone from the logo.
  • The day counter is B's calendar chip (Day 5 of 30, with "25 days left" under it) in A's paper, navy and ochre.
  • Stamps are D's round code stamps (outer ring, dashed inner ring, CPH or PAR, and a check mark) in ochre and navy ink.
  • D's passport strip is always on the map screen: five slots, labelled with the stamp count.
Section 1

Where does the game mode live?

The three phones are the same map screen. The only difference is where the mode label sits. In each one the mode is written out as a word. Under each phone you can see how the same spot looks in Nomad, the harder mode. Nomad uses a filled navy look with a mountain mark, and Explorer is outlined with a compass. Nothing is red and nothing warns.

Section 2

City day, with the stamp at the end

You don't get the stamp when the day starts. First you plan the day: the stay plus 0 to 2 sights. Then you tap "End the day", and the stamp lands in your passport as the closing moment.

These two frames use the M1 header badge for the mode. The final screens will put the mode wherever you choose in Section 1.

What changes in dark mode
  • The paper becomes deep navy (#0F1B26) and the cards become #172634.
  • Ochre gets lighter (#E0A243) so the stamp rings, icons and selected route keep their contrast.
  • The stamp ink flips to cream. The code and the check mark still show the stamp state, so it isn't carried by colour alone.
  • The selected row keeps its thicker border and filled radio, not just a colour change.
