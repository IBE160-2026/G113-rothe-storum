The rubric found two high gaps that would push guesswork onto story-dev: “navy” components that collapse in dark mode because they bind to primary/ink, which flip to ochre and cream (1.0:1), and a Call home sheet with no defined trigger and no persistent entry after “Not now”.

Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

“The visual floor is unusually solid. I recomputed every pair in DESIGN.md → Contrast targets and all of them are correct.” The gaps are behavioural: the spines do not yet define dialog semantics and focus containment, the plan to hide the map from screen readers contradicts itself, the world map's markers break the 44 px floor outside the loupe, the dark-mode flip of primary to ochre collides with every component that puts ochre on primary, and fixed-height layout has no strategy for 200% text. “None of these block sign-off of the visual identity, but the five High items should be fixed in the spines before architecture and story breakdown use them.”

Reviewer counts: Critical 0 · High 5 · Medium 9 · Low 12. Off-table pairs it measured: ochre route dash 2.42:1 on light map-land, ochre-light on dark primary 1.00:1, on-navy on dark primary 1.94:1, cover-explorer-dark vs background-dark 1.08:1, unavailable fill vs surface 1.27:1.

H1 — Modal sheets lack dialog semantics, focus containment and inert background

EXPERIENCE → Component Patterns → Modal sheet; Accessibility Floor → Focus
Focus "moves into the sheet and returns to the trigger", but there is no role="dialog", aria-modal, accessible name, focus trap or inert background. Keyboard and screen-reader users can leave the sheet for controls behind the scrim (WCAG 2.4.3). The resting panel looks like a dialog but isn't, and the spine doesn't separate them.
Fix: role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby to the headline; rest of the app inert; Tab wraps; background scroll locked; initial focus on the headline (tabindex="-1"). State that the resting panel is a normal region (section with heading).

H3 — World-map destination markers break the 44 px floor outside the loupe

DESIGN → Region loupe, Zoomed region, Components "Map"; EXPERIENCE → Content Assets, Component Patterns → Map, Accessibility Floor → Targets

Developer / implementability review

review-developer.md

“The visual system is unusually concrete: every token reference resolves, every colour role has a dark pair, and the component table is close to buildable.
