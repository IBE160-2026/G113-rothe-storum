Screens · extension set 1 · built on Direction A3 and Core 1

Extension screens, first pass

These frames cover the MVP extension: traveller name, mode choice, save and continue, accommodation tiers, night departures and work shifts. They use the approved language: warm paper, navy ink and ochre, the WORLDBOUND wordmark, the calendar Day chip, round code stamps, and the passport cover as the only place the mode appears. The tagline is "Round trip or one-way ticket?"

  • Ingrid (UJ-1, Explorer): she's in Paris on Day 5 with €1,270 and 1 stamp.
  • Amira (UJ-3, Explorer): she's in Tokyo on Day 18 with €240 and 4 stamps. She spent a Comfort city day in Tokyo and visited Senso-ji. Sydney costs €300.
Section 1 · FR-1, FR-18, FR-20

Start: name, mode and continue

On a first visit you get one short form: an optional name, a choice of two trips, and go. If a journey is saved, it comes first, shown as a passport. If a save can't be read, one calm line explains what happened, and the same form follows.

Section 2 · FR-17, FR-23

City day: how does your day end?

The single guesthouse line from CD1 becomes one three-way choice: Budget stay, Comfort stay, or leave tonight. It's one decision with one tap, and each option says what it costs and what it changes. Ingrid is in Paris on Day 5 with €1,270. Budget is €80 × 0.7 = €56, and Comfort is €80 × 1.3 = €104.

Section 3 · FR-15, FR-16, UJ-3

Work a shift

Amira is in Tokyo on Day 18 with €240 and 4 stamps. Sydney costs €300. She stayed Comfort in Tokyo, so her shifts here are 5 questions at €24 each. A shift costs 1 day and no stay. There's no timer anywhere: the questions are a light quiz, not a test.
