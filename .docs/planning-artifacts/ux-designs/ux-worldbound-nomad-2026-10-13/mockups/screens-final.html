Call home, the failed ending, and dark mode

These frames close the last gaps that only existed in round-1 renders or in the spines: the Call home sheet (redrawn on the current map, strip and loupe), its "can't fit" variant, the failed-journey summary, and the first full dark-mode map and route sheet. They follow the five latest memlog decisions: Day = days used + 1, every piece of text is 11 px or more, the cover mode row is 11 px, and stamp codes are IATA codes.

  • Ingrid (UJ-1, Explorer), dark mode: in Paris on Day 6 of 30 with €1,270, with Copenhagen stamped.
