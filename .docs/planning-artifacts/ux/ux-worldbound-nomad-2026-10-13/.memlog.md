## Design alternatives considered

Only the first entry is a human decision. The others are AI proposals, pending team review (see ux-spec §1.1).

- (decision) Map — schematic route map (chosen by Thomas) vs. geographic map with a zoomable Europe inset. Reason: six of nine places are in Europe; at 375 px a geographic map cannot give each a 44 × 44 px tap target (NFR-5) without zooming. The schematic map is easier to read and tap and shows the route network directly. Given up: geographic realism.
- (decision) City view sketched in two variants (A: choose sights before confirming the city day; B: city day first, then sights) with a comparison table, instead of choosing one. Reason: human decision to leave this to Christian.
- (proposal) Destination list (S2b) added as an alternative view of the map, for screen readers, very small screens, and list-preferring players. Alternative considered: map only.
- (proposal) Travel selection as a bottom sheet over the map vs. a separate full screen. Reason: keeps the map context visible and puts actions in the thumb zone. Larger screens use a side panel instead.
- (proposal) Travel is two steps — select a transport mode, then confirm — vs. one tap per mode. Reason: PRD §7 "consequences are stated plainly before the player commits".
- (proposal) After-action preview ("After: €1,063 · Day 8 of 30") on every option that costs money or days. Reason: makes the time-versus-money trade-off visible, and gives time some weight in the core although PRD §9.3 says time is roomy there.
- (proposal) Status bar as a fixed two-line bar on every in-journey screen vs. on the map only (the PRD minimum in FR-3). Reason: the player should never have to return to the map to see money or days.
- (proposal) Change feedback in the status bar after each action (e.g. "€1,153 (−€117)").
